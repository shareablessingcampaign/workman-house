Workman Chat – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • Your chats, photos and settings are stored on your phone. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your data.
  • Bluetooth messages go directly from phone to phone.
  • Internet messages and calls use a free Firebase database that you or your group set up. Messages wait there only until the other phone collects them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in Workman Chat.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Please do not use the app for highly sensitive information.

3. Bluetooth chat

When two phones are connected over Bluetooth, messages and files travel directly between them. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

4. Internet chat and calls (Firebase)

If you turn on internet chat, the app connects to a Google Firebase Realtime Database using the Web API key and Database URL entered in the app. That project belongs to whoever created it, not to us. The app signs in to it anonymously (no personal account).

  • A message sent over the internet is placed in the receiver’s inbox in that database, together with your Workman ID, your display name and a timestamp. The receiving phone collects it and the app then deletes it from the inbox.
  • Voice and video calls use WebRTC. Call set-up information goes through the same database, and the audio/video then flows directly between phones. If a call relay (TURN) is configured, call traffic may pass through that relay. Free Google STUN servers are used to help phones find each other.
  • Google processes data in Firebase under its own terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Whoever runs the Firebase project can see the database contents while messages are waiting. Only use a project run by someone you trust.

5. Gemini AI (optional)

If you add your own Gemini API key and chat with “Gemini AI”, the text you send to that chat (and, if you use “Suggest replies” or “Summarize chat”, the recent messages of that chat) is sent to Google’s Gemini service to get an answer. This is done with your own key and is subject to Google’s terms: policies.google.com/privacy. If you do not add a key, nothing is sent.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth – to find and pair with phones, make your phone visible when you ask, and chat over Bluetooth.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) – Android requires it to search for Bluetooth devices. The app does not read or store your location.
  • Microphone – for voice notes and calls.
  • Camera – for video calls and taking photos.
  • Network state / internet – for internet chat, calls and Gemini.
  • Photos and files – only the items you pick to send.

You can turn any permission off in Android Settings → Apps → Workman Chat → Permissions. Features that need it will stop working.

7. Security

Data is kept in your phone’s app storage and is protected by your phone’s own security. The optional chat lock hides chats behind a PIN on your device. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot promise they are private from the people who run the Firebase project or from anyone who can access your unlocked phone.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your information, show ads, or share it with advertisers. Data leaves your phone only as described above: to the person you message, to the Firebase project you configured, and to Google if you use Gemini.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Chats stay on your phone until you delete them.
  • To remove everything: open ⋮ → Settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Messages in the internet inbox are deleted once the other phone collects them. Messages that were already delivered to a friend’s phone are on their phone and we cannot remove them.
  • Because we hold no accounts or servers of our own, we have no copy of your data to delete. To remove data held in a Firebase project, contact whoever runs it.

10. Children

Workman Chat is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
